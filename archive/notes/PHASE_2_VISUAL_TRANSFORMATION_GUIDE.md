## 📱 RESPONSIVE BEHAVIOR

### Mobile (375px) to Desktop (1280px+)

#### Heading Text Sizing
- **Mobile**: text-3xl = 30px
- **Tablet**: transitions smoothly
- **Desktop**: text-5xl = 48px
- **Result**: Scales beautifully across all devices

#### Label Sizing
- **Mobile**: text-xs = 12px (always)
- **Tablet**: text-xs = 12px (always)
- **Desktop**: text-xs = 12px (always)
- **Result**: Consistent label size across all breakpoints

#### Decorative Line
- **All devices**: h-1 w-16 (4px × 64px)
- **Result**: Maintains visual proportion on all screens
