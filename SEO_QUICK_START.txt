STEP 1: Set Up Google Search Console (5 min)
───────────────────────────────────────────
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with Google account (or create free account)
3. Click "Add property" or "Create property"
4. Enter: https://yoniserd.co.ke
5. Choose: "URL prefix" method
6. Google will give you a meta tag → Copy it
7. Contact your hosting provider (TrueHost/Hostinger) to add the meta tag
   OR ask us for help adding it
8. Click "Verify" in Google Search Console

STEP 2: Submit Your Sitemap (1 min)
───────────────────────────────────
1. In Google Search Console, click "Sitemaps" (left menu)
2. Enter: sitemap.xml
3. Click "Submit"
4. Done! Google will now crawl your site

STEP 3: Monitor Progress (Ongoing)
──────────────────────────────────
1. Check "Coverage" section → See which pages are indexed
2. Check "Performance" section → See search results & clicks
3. Wait 2-3 weeks for initial indexing
4. Add new content to improve rankings
